Captured pedals
===============

Toneical's drive, distortion and fuzz pedals play these neural captures of real pedals.
The pedal names in Toneical are playful sound-alikes; product names below only say
what was captured and belong to their owners.

From the Neural Amp Modeler community collection
https://github.com/pelennor2170/NAM_models  (GNU GPL v3, see assets/nam/COPYING-GPL-3.txt)
  klon_g6.nam / klon_max.nam   Keith B   - Klon Centaur clone, gain 6 / max   -> Clone Kentaur
  sd1.nam                      Phillipe P - Boss SD-1                         -> Baby SD-1
  ds1_g6.nam / ds1_max.nam     Keith B   - Boss DS-1, dist 6 / max            -> Baby DS-1
  os2.nam                      Helga B   - Boss OS-2                          -> Baby OS-2
  hm2_*.nam                    Peter N   - Boss HM-2, four settings           -> Baby HM-2
  m77.nam / m77_bump.nam       Tudor N   - MXR M77 Custom Badass Modified OD  -> MXD Bad Mod 77
  precision.nam                Phillipe P - Horizon Devices Precision Drive   -> Precise Drive
  grind.nam                    Tudor N   - Fortin Grind                       -> Fortune Grind
  sd805.nam                    Tim R     - Seymour Duncan 805                 -> Seemore 805

From the GuitarML ToneLibrary (Proteus models)
https://github.com/GuitarML/ToneLibrary  (GNU GPL v3, see LICENSE-ToneLibrary-GPL-3.txt)
  ts9_drive.json               Ibanez TS9, Drive knob learned               -> Green Screamer
  rat.json                     ProCo RAT, high gain                         -> Rodent
  bigmuff.json                 Electro-Harmonix Little Big Muff             -> Little Big Fluff
  mxr78.json                   MXR '78 Custom Badass Distortion, knob learned -> XD '78 Distortion
  mt2.json                     Boss MT-2 Metal Zone, high gain              -> Baby MT-2
  xoticbb.json                 Xotic BB Preamp, Gain knob learned           -> Exotic BB Preamp
  protein_blue/green.json      Browne Protein, both sides, Drive learned    -> Proteen Dual Drive

Files were renamed, otherwise unchanged. GuitarML models are trained at 44.1 kHz; Toneical
resamples around them, like the Proteus plugin does.
