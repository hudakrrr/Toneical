Real Amp captures
=================

The "Real Amp" head plays these Neural Amp Modeler (NAM) captures of real amplifiers.
They come from the community collection https://github.com/pelennor2170/NAM_models
(made by members of the Neural Amp Modeler Facebook group), licensed under the
GNU GPL v3 - see COPYING-GPL-3.txt. Files were renamed, otherwise unchanged.

  clean_twin.nam         Tim R  - "Tim R Fender TwinVerb Norm Bright.nam"
  crunch_jcm2000.nam     Tim R  - "Tim R JCM2000 Crunch.nam"
  lead_jcm2000_g6.nam    Tim R  - "Tim R JCM2000 L2 G6.nam"
  lead_jcm2000_g8.nam    Tim R  - "Tim R JCM2000 L2 G8.nam"
  chime_ac15.nam         Phillipe P - "Phillipe P VOXAC15-TopBoost.nam"
  highgain_5150.nam      Helga B - "Helga B 5150 BlockLetter - NoBoost.nam"

All are amp-head captures (no cabinet): put a Cab Sim or IR Loader after the Real Amp.
Product names belong to their owners and are used only to say what was captured.
